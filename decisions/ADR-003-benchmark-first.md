# ADR-003: Benchmark-First Claims

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-07-28

## Context

Visual demonstrations can be persuasive while remaining statistically weak.

## Decision

Benchmarks will pre-register expected behaviour, baselines, metrics and failure conditions before final interpretation.

## Consequences

Experiments become more credible and reproducible. Some early results may remain inconclusive rather than being presented as successes.
